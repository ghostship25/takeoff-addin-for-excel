Getting started

  1. On the Home tab, choose Takeoff to open the pane.
  2. Choose Open drawing and pick a DXF or PDF file.
  3. Under Scale, pick a paper scale or calibrate with two points, and choose where it applies (this page, a region, chosen pages or all pages).
  4. Measure with Length, Area, Count or Symbols, then Send to put the quantity in the selected cell as a formula.

Measuring

Length: straight line, polyline, arc (3 points), curve (Bézier), spline, freehand. Area: polygon, rectangle, rotated rectangle, circle, ellipse, sector, arc segment, freehand. Arcs, circles, ellipses and sectors use exact formulas. Count: markers, or posts at a spacing along a path. Pick measures an existing vector line or closed shape exactly from the drawing. Angle and Symbols (find look-alikes of a boxed symbol) are also available.

In Pan mode, click an item to select it and drag its square handles to adjust it. Right or middle mouse drag pans.

Civil and BOQ quantities

Open an item's Details to set a code, turn a length into an area or volume (× width, × depth), turn an area into a perimeter or volume, mark an opening as a deduction, repeat it ×N, add waste %, and set a rate. Amounts are calculated in Excel when you send a table with the Rate and Amount columns.

Choosing where quantities land

Under Send to Excel: where and how, choose the selected cell or a fixed cell, which way to move after each send, whether to write just the quantity or label + quantity + unit, which table columns to include, and whether a table goes at the destination or on a new Takeoff sheet. Use Link to selected cell on an item to tie it to one exact cell of your own BOQ, then Update linked cells to refresh them all after a scale change.

Common questions

My quantities are off. Presets assume the PDF is printed at the sheet size the scale was drawn for. If the sheet was shrunk, use Calibrate with a known dimension, or use “Check against a known size” after a Distance measurement.

DWG files. Save the drawing as ASCII DXF first.

Scanned PDFs. These have no vector lines to total, so use the measuring tools and Symbols.
