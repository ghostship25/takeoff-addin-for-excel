Takeoff: privacy policy

Last updated: {{DATE}}

Your drawings stay on your device

Drawings you open (DXF and PDF files) are read and measured inside the add-in in your own browser or Excel session. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not receive them.

What the add-in stores

Scales and takeoff items you create are saved with your workbook (and, where Excel is unavailable, in your browser's local storage) so they come back when you reopen the same file. This data stays in your workbook or browser. We do not collect it.

What the add-in sends to Excel

Only the quantities you choose to send are written into the selected cells of your workbook.

Third parties

The add-in loads Microsoft's Office.js library from Microsoft. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
